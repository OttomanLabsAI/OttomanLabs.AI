Most small businesses end up with one of two websites: a template that looks like every other template, or a four-figure agency build that took months and still needs a developer every time the phone number changes. I build the third kind — designed around your business, written by hand, and handed over in days. Whether that means starting from a blank page or taking the site you already have and rebuilding it properly, the work is the same standard and the price is agreed before it starts.

Why it costs what it costs

The price is not low because the work is small. It is low because the method is fast. I build with AI as a working partner — the same approach behind the engineering tools on the rest of this site — so the month of billable design and front-end hours an agency would put on the invoice takes days here. You get those hours back as price and speed; I get to take on more of the work I enjoy. What doesn’t change is the part that matters: every page is still art-directed by hand, to the last hairline, by someone who cares whether the spacing is right.
